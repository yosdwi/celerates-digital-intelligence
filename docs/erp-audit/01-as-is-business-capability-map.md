# 01 — As-is business capability map

See [scope and evidence rules](README.md). The code implements a substantial operational ERP, with several incomplete control and recovery paths. It is neither a static prototype nor a validated production service.

Status vocabulary: **A** implemented, apparently usable from code; **B** implemented but operationally incomplete; **P** explicit placeholder; **T** transitional/duplicated; **U** requires stakeholder or live-data validation. All A capabilities still inherit the deployment/security gates in [04](04-production-readiness-audit.md).

## Cross-domain journeys to preserve and validate

1. **Lead → opportunity → requisition/PQ.** Marketing editor qualifies a lead, Sales converts a tracker to requisition and PQ. Preserve the existing conversion transaction; test repeat/concurrent conversion and whether role, headcount, level and price-period survive. Evidence: `convertLeadToOpportunity`, `convertToRequisition`. Exit: one linked chain with approved field mapping, plus rejection of unauthorized actors.
2. **Candidate → onboarding → employee; PQ approval → downstream setup.** TA creates onboarding, a nominated signer approves PQ, TA promotes employee. Exercise both orders of signing/promotion. Evidence: `promoteToEmployee`, `onPqSigned`, `onTalentPromoted`. Exit: one employee/contract/BPJS pair, recoverable TM/PMO notification and setup, no duplicate side effects on retry.
3. **Talent assignment → extension → commercial change.** Existing approval journey schedules signatures and supports explicit owner override. Evidence: `ERP:src/lib/approval-journey.ts` — `startExtensionRequestJourney`, `advanceExtensionRequestJourney`, `ownerOverrideExtensionRequestJourney`. Exit: approved signer sequence, preserved before/after salary and contract values, rejected/stale approvals cannot advance.
4. **Contract → monthly invoice → PMO document handoff → Finance acknowledgement/revision.** Evidence: `generateMonthlyBillings`, `upsertFinanceHandoff`, `acknowledgeFinanceHandoff`, `requestRevision`. Exit: agreed invoice/handoff cardinality, two months tracked independently, mismatch IDs rejected, duplicate generation harmless, BA confirms overdue and proration rules.
5. **Talent attendance/timesheet → approver → correction.** Evidence: `checkIn`, `checkOut`, `approveTimesheetSubmission`, `approveCurrentStep`. Exit: correct local work date, owned records only, designated approver only, evidence retained after corrections; user↔employee mapping is explicit.
6. **Reviewer feedback → BA delta → release → reporter validation.** Extend existing tracker as [05](05-user-feedback-and-requirement-loop.md); test as talent, viewer, editor and BA. A feedback form alone is not an accepted loop.

## Proposed pilot boundary

R: begin with one Marketing, Sales, TA, HR/TM, PMO, Finance and BA representative plus two synthetic talent accounts; actual names and number must be assigned by the sponsor. Use synthetic/sanitized records first. Include the six journeys above only as their security/data gates pass. Keep live Sheets push, automatic outbound reminders and WhatsApp disabled until their dedicated gates pass. This sequencing preserves the business surface without asserting unverified end-to-end readiness.
