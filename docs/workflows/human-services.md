# Human Services

## Purpose

Help talent/employees get faster support without forcing nuanced internal service into a chatbot-only experience.

The Intelligence Layer helps understand the request, gather context, retrieve policy/knowledge, route cases, and prepare drafts. Human PICs remain visible and responsible when the issue requires judgement or action.

## Surfaces

- ERP/web Human Service inbox;
- contextual copilot;
- WhatsApp assistant;
- in-app notification;
- optional email/Teams notification.

A WhatsApp conversation is a channel, not the canonical case record.

## Workflow

1. Employee/talent asks for help.
2. Resolve user identity and authorized context.
3. Classify request/intent and required domain (Talent/HR/Finance/etc.).
4. Retrieve relevant policy/knowledge and ERP facts.
5. Determine whether the request is routine/safe to answer directly.
6. If yes: return concise answer with source/evidence.
7. If not: create a case and route to the correct PIC.
8. Prepare a context pack: user/project/transaction/policy/evidence.
9. Optionally prepare a response/next-action draft.
10. Human PIC reviews and responds/acts.
11. Record outcome and resolution state.
12. Curate recurring patterns into improved knowledge/service flow; do not auto-train blindly from every conversation.

## Human Service Case Pack

- requester;
- category/intent;
- question/request;
- relevant ERP facts;
- policy/knowledge sources;
- suggested PIC;
- suggested next action;
- draft response;
- case status;
- action/resolution history.

## Principle

The system should reduce the PIC’s context-gathering work. It should not hide the human service relationship behind a generic bot.
